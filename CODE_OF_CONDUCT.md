# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims with evidence, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of another person's secrets or private data are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project contact that will be published before the first public beta.
